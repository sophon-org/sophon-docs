---
title: "Farming FAQs (Legacy)"
description: "Current answers for the concluded Sophon Farm"
---

<Accordion title="Is Sophon farming still active?">
  No. The programme ended on June 30, 2025, and no new deposits, locks, boosts, or
  Sophon Points are available through these docs.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I withdraw a remaining balance?">
  Start at [claim.sophon.com](https://claim.sophon.com), select **Withdraw from
  Sophon**, and review the routes supplied through Jumper.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I recover a permanently boosted amount?">
  Under the original rules, boosted assets were permanently committed to an
  ownerless liquidity position and the associated LP tokens were burned. The
  wind-down does not change those original position terms.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do Sophon Points still accrue?">
  No. The points programme has concluded. Historical points informed the published
  farming reward allocations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I get help?">
  Use [Sophon's official Discord or X account](/links/socials). Never trust an
  unsolicited farm-migration or claim link.
</Accordion>
