<Warning>
  Sophon farming ended on **June 30, 2025**. Do not deposit or bridge assets into
  Sophon based on this archive. Use [claim.sophon.com](https://claim.sophon.com) to
  review current balances and withdrawal routes.
</Warning>

The Sophon Farm was a two-phase liquidity-mining programme. It began on Ethereum
before the Sophon mainnet launch, then moved deposited assets and reward activity to
the Sophon Chain.

Participants earned Sophon Points, and the published token allocation assigned 6%
of the original SOPH issuance to the pre-mainnet phase and 2% to the post-mainnet
phase. These programmes are concluded.

The remaining pages in this section preserve the former rules for historical
reference only.
