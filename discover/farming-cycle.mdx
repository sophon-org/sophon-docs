## Ethereum phase

The original farm accepted deposits on Ethereum and awarded Sophon Points. Before
the Sophon mainnet phase, eligible deposited assets were moved to Sophon.

## Sophon phase

After the December 2024 mainnet launch, farming and withdrawals moved to the Sophon
Chain. New boosts were disabled, and points from the programme informed the
published SOPH reward allocations.

The Sophon farming programme ended on June 30, 2025. This timeline is not an active
deposit or reward guide.
