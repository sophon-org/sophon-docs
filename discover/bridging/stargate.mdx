---
title: "Stargate (Retired)"
description: "Retired LayerZero route for the Sophon Chain"
---

<Warning>
  Sophon was removed from the LayerZero network on **June 25, 2026**. The former
  Stargate route to or from Sophon is no longer supported.
</Warning>

To move SOPH, withdraw from Sophon to Ethereum through
[claim.sophon.com](https://claim.sophon.com), then use LayerZero from Ethereum to
another supported chain if needed.
