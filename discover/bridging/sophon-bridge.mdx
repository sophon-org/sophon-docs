title: "Canonical Withdrawal"
description: "Withdraw SOPH and supported assets from Sophon to Ethereum"
---

<Warning>
  The canonical bridge no longer accepts deposits into Sophon. Use this route only
  to withdraw from Sophon to Ethereum.
</Warning>

## Withdraw through the portal

1. Go to [claim.sophon.com](https://claim.sophon.com).
2. Connect the wallet that holds your Sophon balance.
3. Select **Withdraw from Sophon** for the asset.
4. Choose the canonical Ethereum route when it is offered.
5. Review the token, amount, destination address, and fees.
6. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.
7. Return to the portal when the withdrawal is ready to be finalized or claimed on
   Ethereum.

Canonical ZK withdrawals are not immediate. Sophon batches must be finalized and
proven on Ethereum before a withdrawal can be completed. See
[Withdrawal Delay](/discover/bridging/withdrawal-delay) for the technical background.

<Info>
  The portal may route non-SOPH assets through Jumper instead when another supported
  route is more suitable.
</Info>
