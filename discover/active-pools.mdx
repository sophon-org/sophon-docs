<Warning>
  There are no active pools documented here. Do not send tokens to a former farm or
  pool contract. Use [claim.sophon.com](https://claim.sophon.com) to review and
  withdraw remaining Sophon balances.
</Warning>

The Sophon Farm previously supported multiple asset pools and, where available,
converted deposits to yield-bearing representations. The programme ended on June
30, 2025, so the former contract list has been removed from current guidance.
