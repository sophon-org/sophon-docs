---
title: "Operator Addresses (Retired)"
description: "Archived status of Sophon light-node operator addresses"
---

<Warning>
  Operator addresses and delegation are no longer required for Guardian rewards.
  Sophon light-node software has been retired.
</Warning>

Operator addresses previously identified light-node operators, received Guardian
delegations, and tracked operator commissions. Do not create a new operator address
or change delegation for the purpose of preserving rewards during the chain migration.

Guardian rewards continue on the original schedule without software or delegation
requirements. Claim available rewards at
[claim.sophon.com](https://claim.sophon.com).
