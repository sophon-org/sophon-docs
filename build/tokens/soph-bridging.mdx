## Current architecture

SOPH is an ERC-20 token native to Ethereum. The token at the system address on the
Sophon Chain is a canonically bridged representation, not a separate token that
needs to be migrated.

As part of the chain wind-down, an Ethereum OFT adapter replaced the Sophon
NativeOFTAdapter. The Ethereum adapter locks ERC-20 SOPH; it requires token
approval before sending. SOPH can also move directly between the other supported
LayerZero chains.

## Supported flows

- **Sophon → Ethereum:** use the canonical withdrawal route through
  [claim.sophon.com](https://claim.sophon.com).
- **Between Ethereum, Base, BNB Smart Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon and Beam:** follow
  [Bridge SOPH with LayerZero](/build/tokens/layerzero_integration) for contract
  addresses, endpoint IDs, approval, fee quoting and sending with the official CLI
  or a contract explorer.
- **Sophon → another chain:** first withdraw SOPH canonically to Ethereum, then use
  LayerZero from Ethereum to the supported destination.

The following flows are no longer supported:

- Ethereum → Sophon deposits;
- LayerZero transfers to or from Sophon.

## Exchange and bridge integrations

For partners providing SOPH exchange or bridge services, the June 25 replacement
of the Sophon NativeOFTAdapter with an Ethereum OFT adapter is the announced operational change.
Existing non-Sophon routes otherwise remain unchanged. Do not treat the legacy
Sophon adapter as an active endpoint.

See the [Sophon Chain migration guide](/migration) for other assets, managed
positions, and the transition timeline.
