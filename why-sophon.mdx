Sophon is now a technology studio that builds and operates onchain consumer
products, rather than a general-purpose blockchain ecosystem. The products are
being built on Base.

## Product focus

Sophon is focusing on onchain consumer products. Its first announced application in
this model is the upcoming **Pyre** neobank. Pyre's announced revenue streams include
card interchange, vault performance fees, and reserve yield from PYRUSD.

This product-led model also changes how SOPH is designed to accrue utility. A portion
of profits from Sophon products will fund open-market SOPH purchases, and purchased
tokens will be permanently burned rather than redistributed.
