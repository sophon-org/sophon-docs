Sophon has evolved from a general-purpose ZK chain into a technology studio building
onchain consumer experiences. Its applications are being built on Base, while the
Sophon Chain is being wound down in a planned transition.

<Warning>
  New deposits into the Sophon Chain have been disabled since **June 25, 2026**.
  If you hold assets on Sophon, review the [migration guide](/migration) and withdraw
  them before the final decommissioning date.
</Warning>

These docs now serve three purposes:

- explain the Sophon Chain migration and asset-withdrawal process;
- document SOPH's transition to a product-revenue-funded buyback-and-burn model;
- preserve the Sophon v1 chain documentation as a legacy technical reference.
